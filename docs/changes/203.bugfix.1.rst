``HENzsearch --fast`` now charges the number of independent trials measured
with Monte Carlo simulations of pure noise, instead of one trial per resolution
element: neighbouring points of an oversampled grid are correlated, but not
completely. Its detection threshold, and the targeted search around a known
ephemeris, use this calibrated count. The candidate table always reports the
single-trial probability (``p_1trial``) and the probability corrected with the
naive (``p_ntrial``) and calibrated (``p_ntrial_adj``) number of trials.
