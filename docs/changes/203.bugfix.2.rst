``HENaccelsearch`` counted one trial per frequency bin, ignoring the
acceleration (z) axis and interbinning. It now charges the number of trials
measured with Monte Carlo simulations of pure noise (e.g. 0.9 per frequency bin
per z row with ``--delta-z 1``), and gives the in-between bins of ``--interbin``
their own, wider, noise distribution. Every candidate reports its single-trial
probability (``p_1trial``) and the probability corrected with the naive
(``p_ntrial``) and calibrated (``p_ntrial_adj``) number of trials. Blind
searches only write candidates whose ``p_ntrial_adj`` is below the detection
threshold, which removes many noise candidates that used to be reported.
